Arrow Escape — Privacy Policy Last updated: November 2, 2026 Arrow Escape is an Android puzzle game. This policy explains how information is handled when you play. Game progress Arrow Escape stores gameplay progress, including levels, coins, hearts, and preferences, locally on your device. Clearing app data may reset your progress. Advertising Arrow Escape integrates Google AdMob to display banner advertisements and optional rewarded videos. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising interactions, and diagnostic data to deliver, measure, and protect advertisements. For more information, see "Google's Privacy Policy" (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Advertising consent and choices Where required, Arrow Escape requests advertising consent. You can manage available advertising preferences through the game's privacy settings and your Android device settings. Declining optional personalized advertising does not prevent you from playing Arrow Escape. Children Arrow Escape is not specifically directed to children. Data retention Local gameplay information remains on your device until cleared or removed, subject to device backup settings. Third-party advertising data is governed by the relevant provider's policies. Policy changes We may update this policy as the game develops. Updates will be published on this page. Contact For privacy questions, contact the developer through the "Arrow Escape GitHub repository" (https://github.com/Subhamalwar/arrow-bottles/issues). Do not post sensitive personal information in public issues.
